Waymark · Privacy policy

How your data is kept

Effective 4 November 2026

Waymark is a trip planner for a small group travelling together: you plan stops, pack, and leave each other notes. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

Who runs Waymark

Waymark is developed and operated by Luis Ortiz, publishing as LFOrtiz, who is the developer and operator responsible for the data described here. For any privacy question or request, write to me@lfortiz.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password, which is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Your profile: the display name you choose and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Trips you create or join: trip names, destinations, dates and cover photos; itinerary stops (titles, places, times, notes, status); packing items and who is bringing them.
  • Notes: shared notes, which every member of that trip can read, and private notes, which only you can read.
  • Invite codes you create to bring people onto a trip, when they expire, and when and by whom they were used.
  • Sign-in records: like any online service, the sign-in provider's servers log technical details such as IP address, device or browser type and sign-in times, to keep accounts secure.
  • On your device only: the schedule of trip reminders and your light/dark preference. These never leave your phone.
  • Offline copy: so Waymark works without a connection, your phone also keeps a copy of your trips (never the text of private notes) and any changes not yet sent. Signing out deletes it.

Waymark has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your data for marketing. Photos are only read when you pick one from your library.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to create your account and sign you in, to show each trip to its members, to keep everyone's changes in sync, to send the emails you ask for, such as a password reset link, and to remind you about your own trips.

Who can see what

  • Members of a trip see that trip's details, stops, packing list, shared notes, and each other's display name and photo.
  • Your private notes are visible only to you — not to other members, and not to the trip owner.
  • Other members never see your email address.
  • People who are not members of a trip cannot see anything in it. Invite codes are single-use and expire. This is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app.

Who processes it

Waymark stores data with Supabase, which provides the app's sign-in, database and file storage, and handles your data only on our behalf. Data travels between the app and Supabase encrypted with TLS. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no push service is involved.

Waymark sends account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset and email-address changes) through Amazon Simple Email Service (Amazon SES), provided by Amazon Web Services in the United States. Amazon SES receives your email address and the message content to deliver them.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account:

  • your account, profile, profile photo and every note you wrote (private and shared) are erased;
  • trips you created that have other members pass to the member who joined first, with their content, so your travel companions don't lose their plans;
  • trips where you were the only member are deleted, including their cover photos;
  • stops and packing items you added to other people's trips stay on those trips, without your name;
  • your invite codes are deleted, and your reminders, your sign-in and the offline copy of your trips are removed from the device you deleted from.

Deleted data can remain for a short time in our provider's routine backups until those are replaced. Our provider's sign-in logs (such as IP address and sign-in times) are kept for a limited period for security.

If an email to your address bounced or was reported as spam, Amazon SES may keep the address on a suppression list so that no further email is sent to it. Email delivery logs are kept by our providers for a limited period.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Without the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page, or email me@lfortiz.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Children

Waymark is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used: write to me@lfortiz.com. Depending on where you live, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date, and important changes will be announced in the app first.
