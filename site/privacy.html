Waymark · Privacy policy

How your data is kept

Effective 25 October 2026

Waymark is a trip planner for a small group travelling together: you plan stops, pack, and leave each other notes. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

Who runs Waymark

Waymark is developed and operated by its independent developer. For any privacy question or request, write to me@lfortiz.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password, which is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Your profile: the display name you choose and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Trips you create or join: trip names, destinations, dates and cover photos; itinerary stops (titles, places, times, notes, status); packing items and who is bringing them.
  • Notes: shared notes, which every member of that trip can read, and private notes, which only you can read.
  • Invite codes you create to bring people onto a trip, and when they were used.
  • On your device only: the schedule of trip reminders and your light/dark preference. These never leave your phone.
  • Offline copy: so Waymark works without a connection, your phone also keeps a copy of your trips (never the text of private notes) and any changes not yet sent. Signing out deletes it.

Waymark has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your data for marketing. Photos are only read when you pick one from your library.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to show each trip to its members, to keep everyone's changes in sync, and to remind you about your own trips.

Who can see what

  • Members of a trip see that trip's details, stops, packing list, shared notes, and each other's display name and photo.
  • Your private notes are visible only to you — not to other members, and not to the trip owner.
  • People who are not members of a trip cannot see anything in it. This is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app.

Who processes it

Waymark stores data with Supabase, which provides the app's sign-in, database and file storage. Data travels between the app and Supabase encrypted with TLS.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account:

  • your account, profile, profile photo and every note you wrote (private and shared) are erased;
  • trips you created that have other members pass to the member who joined first, with their content, so your travel companions don't lose their plans;
  • trips where you were the only member are deleted, including their cover photos;
  • stops and packing items you added to other people's trips stay on those trips, without your name.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Without the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Children

Waymark is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date, and important changes will be announced in the app.
