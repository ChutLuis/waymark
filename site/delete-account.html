Waymark · Account deletion

Delete your Waymark account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Waymark and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@waymark.app from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Waymark account”. You don't need to reinstall the app. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details, display name and profile photo.
  • Every note you wrote, private and shared.
  • Trips where you were the only member, with their stops, packing lists and cover photos.
  • Your invite codes.

What stays, and why

  • Trips you created that have other members pass to the member who joined first, with their stops, packing list and cover photo, so your travel companions keep their plans.
  • Stops and packing items you added to other people's trips stay on those trips without your name.

Nothing about you is kept after deletion: there are no backups of your profile or notes that we restore later, and no copy is kept for marketing.
